ABOUT UMMAHSALAH
----------------
UmmahSalah is a fast, 100% offline-capable, zero-API-cost Islamic
companion web application built for Denmark, the UK, East Africa, and
Muslim communities worldwide.

KEY FEATURES
------------
1. 67+ Verified Mosques Worldwide (Adhan & Iqamah)
   - Denmark: Copenhagen & outskirts (Masjid Al-Iman, Wakf, Imam Malik,
     Hamad Bin Khalifa, Minhaj-ul-Quran, etc.), Korsor, Roskilde, Koge,
     Aarhus, Odense, Aalborg, Esbjerg, Vejle, Horsens, Herning.
   - United Kingdom: Luton Islamic Centre, East London Mosque.
   - East Africa: Kenya (Mombasa, Nairobi, Malindi, Lamu), Tanzania
     (Tanga, Dar es Salaam, Arusha), Uganda (Kampala, Jinja), Burundi
     (Bujumbura), Rwanda (Kigali).
   - World Mosques: Makkah, Madinah, Paris, Berlin, Stockholm, Oslo,
     Istanbul, Cairo, New York, Los Angeles, San Francisco, Toronto,
     Sydney, Jakarta.
   - Includes 1-click Website links, Google Maps links, Favorites saving,
     and per-mosque Adhan (+/- min) & Iqamah & Jumu'ah Time Wheel editor.

2. Local Astronomical Math & Smart Auto-Detection ($0 API Cost)
   - Calculates all prayer times and Qiblah bearings locally on your
     device with automatic Daylight Saving Time (DST) and High-Latitude
     rules (>= 48 deg: One-Seventh of Night, Middle of Night, Angle-Based).
   - Automatically selects the standard calculation method for your
     country (ISNA, MWL, Umm al-Qura, Diyanet, Egypt) with manual override.

3. Analog & 24-Hour Digital Clock + Qiblah Finder + Sun Projection
   - 4 iOS 27 Glass Light & Pearl layouts.
   - Auto-Luminary glow in dark or poor-light conditions.
   - Embedded Sun Projection arc (Fajr -> Shuruq -> Dhuhr -> Asr ->
     Maghrib -> Isha) directly on the clock face.
   - Qiblah Finder with live compass degrees, +/-5 deg lock & flash,
     and GPS displacement tracking.

4. Perpetual Calendar, Ramadan Countdown, Rafiki AI & Spiritual Habits
   - Perpetual Gregorian & Hijri calendar with regional +/-2 day offset.
   - Rafiki AI multilingual assistant in 7 languages (English, Dansk,
     Arabic, Turkish, Urdu, Swahili, Somali).
   - Colored Tajweed Quran reader & audio (via AlQuran Cloud), Daily
     Adhkar counter, Private Prayer & Qada tracker, and Random Islamic
     Quiz with topic explanations.

HOW TO PUBLISH ONLINE
---------------------
1. Unzip ummahsalah_webhost_pwa.zip into a folder.
2. Upload index.html, manifest.json, sw.js, icon-512.jpg, and README.txt
   to Netlify Drop (https://app.netlify.com/drop), GitHub Pages,
   Cloudflare Pages, or any cPanel public_html folder.

HOW TO INSTALL ON MOBILE
------------------------
- iOS (iPhone/iPad): Open your live https:// URL in Safari -> Share ->
  Add to Home Screen.
- Android: Open in Chrome -> Menu -> Install app, or build the native
  APK using ummahsalah_android_studio.zip in Android Studio.
